# Raw-material foundation — measured outcomes

Generated by `node scripts/report-materials.mjs`; protocol was declared in [MATERIAL_EVALUATION.md](MATERIAL_EVALUATION.md) before results were examined. Implementation scope and missing modern-technology rules are in [MATERIAL_FOUNDATION.md](MATERIAL_FOUNDATION.md).

13 final runs are recorded. All completed at their declared modeled duration with every starting agent alive and every sampled/final checkpoint valid. These small paired samples do not establish better intelligence, unlimited survival or modern technological discovery.

The two arms use the same policy, priors and computational budgets. The geological arm adds finite visible rock sources, so subsequent observations and chosen work can differ. More parts or tests are not scored as success. Negative outcome records and work costs are reported rather than hidden by the equal survival counts.

No tasks, desired hypotheses, learned answers, objects or supplies were injected in these natural runs. A rock's feedstock identity below is observer provenance; the agent has not chemically identified or refined it.

## What the data can and cannot show

Mineral extraction is measured by finite deposit depletion; carried and embodied mass are conserved subsets of stone. Zero extracted stock can be sensible abstention. Physical work excludes travel/metabolism. The logger field `failedActions` actually counts negative outcome RECORDS: a failed physical operation emits a detail event and a general action event, so one attempt can count twice. Refusals use a different `accepted` field and are not counted here. Original JSON notes calling these failed actions are imprecise; the data is retained unchanged and labeled correctly below. The logger note is corrected for future runs. Neither this counter nor repeated-block signatures prove preventability. Checkpoint bytes are sampled every six modeled hours plus initialization/final state; RSS/heap include the Node process and allocator. Timings are shared Windows-desktop Node wall time, not browser worker latency, real-device speed or renderer FPS.

## A natural material-accounting example

In `materials-dev-1`, the unassigned policy collected 4.5 raw units from tungsten_ore. It completed 1 physical part(s), with 2.331318 raw units still embedded. 2.168682 units remained carried and 95.5 remained in the deposit (100 initially). The agent survived 72 modeled hours. This demonstrates ordinary collection and structural use of mineral-bearing rock, not tungsten extraction or chemical discovery.

## Correction and retained initial results

An isolated executor regression exposed two-decimal collection rounding after six-decimal shaping (2.494 units becoming 2.49). Geological-study stone accounting now keeps six decimals. Legacy studies retain their original arithmetic. The first six development runs and their original source fingerprint are retained above; none was discarded or relabeled as an untouched holdout. The complete development matrix was rerun, and held-out evaluation used only the corrected source. The correction was informed by the unit fixture, not a hidden evaluation-seed search.

The UI refinement and final command/browser checks are documented in [MATERIAL_VERIFICATION.md](MATERIAL_VERIFICATION.md).
