## Implemented behavior

Milestone 1 is a bounded protection/structural-support discovery loop, not
general technological invention. Private observations and predicted survival
conditions create goals. Alternatives include ordinary survival, resting,
moving/reusing an observed arrangement, shaping and placing parts, support
prerequisites, and consequential optional physical tests. Real measurements
update contextual expectations; successfully executed sequences can be rebound
and executed with new objects, paying every primitive's costs again.

The observation/action boundary is a runtime allowlist, not just a TypeScript
annotation. World generation, hidden material coefficients, other agents' minds
and observer diagnostics are excluded. A separate deterministic policy random
stream does not disclose the world seed. Evidence keeps original provenance;
testimony does not become a second independent trial. No external model,
credential, cloud execution, chemistry catalog or roadmap stub was introduced.

Designers still supply senses, physiology, primitive operations, fallible
material/geometry priors, survival scoring, finite search and memory budgets.
Agents learn contextual corrections, failed proposals, observed routes and
executed procedures. There is no undisclosed curiosity or building-count reward.
Construction remains optional, and survival is not guaranteed.

## Defects corrected during continuation

1. **Recovery journeys repeatedly interrupted by an existing injury.** Policy 4
   now compares the complete remaining plan with staying exposed over a bounded
   private forecast. It can retain a viable recovery chain despite temporary
   injury, but still interrupts a plan forecast to die before relief. No damage
   rate, supply amount or survival threshold was weakened.
2. **Protection benefit credited during travel.** Complete-plan prices now apply
   destination protection after arrival. Resource collection uses the shared
   observed bank/reach rules and actual primitive yield. Ordinary alternative
   prices use their final destination rather than the departure point.
3. **Precise test point inside a rotated part.** Reuse/test proposals now predict
   and execute at the legal approach returned by private navigation. An adjusted
   contact endpoint cannot masquerade as arrival at a blocked precise point.
   Material acquisition also binds a legal reachable approach. Exhausting a
   project budget records the failed proposal, suppressing an unchanged retry.
4. **Imprecise return to personally measured protection.** New-policy return
   actions use a 0.2-world-unit arrival radius. Legacy behavior is preserved.
5. **Timeline focus lost after leaving the inspector dialog.** The linked entry
   is positioned at its heading and focused after modal cleanup, without moving
   the whole page or covering navigation. This was reproduced and rechecked in
   the actual production preview, not inferred from source alone.
6. **Misleading survivor denominator.** Run now compares living agents with
   actual admitted lives, not the five-slot capacity. A one-agent study reads
   one of one, without implying four agents died or went missing.

Repeated route/immersion prices are cached only within one private decision
snapshot, capped at 256 entries. The cache changes neither search budgets nor
stored agent state; cached and fresh prices are tested for equality. No decision
depends on renderer frame rate or wall-clock deadlines.

not globally yoked after trajectories diverge. The fixed baseline shares the
competent survival planner but restricts construction to one stable proposal;
it is not a fixed script for the whole life. Frozen parameters still retain
failures, evidence and executed procedures. No superiority claim is made from
these small matrices or their different measurement distributions.

## Compatibility and limits

New UI studies use policy 4, schema 5, discovery version 1. Existing policy-1/2/3
studies retain their policy family; no retrospective private measurements or
goals are invented. The public constructor still defaults to policy 2 unless
explicitly configured. Storage fencing, compare-and-swap, archives, exports,
permanent death and the separately configured optional continuity objective are
preserved. The observed public study was paused at Day 1, 03:10 with three lives
before publication; verification must not advance or reset it.

The remaining smallest useful step is to profile and strengthen private
recovery valuation under changed conditions, using common held-out measurements
and matched costs, before adding a second physical domain. Protection sensing is
explicitly supplied, model uncertainty is not a calibrated probability, and
bounded geometry search is not unrestricted invention. Large checkpoints and
multi-agent route searches still incur cost. Browser-local simulation stops
when the browser cannot execute; there is no off-browser advancement promise.
